Questions

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Singing is a skill like any other. Anyone can learn it from scratch. Being a complete beginner is actually a plus: no bad habits to unlearn, just foundations built the right way from day one. If your voice is good enough to speak, it's good enough to sing.

What happens after the first session?

If you want to keep going, we pick what fits at the end of the session. All the options and prices are at the bottom of this page. No pressure either way.

Do you work with songs in other languages?

Yes. I work on how your voice produces sound, and that's the same in any language.

Do I need any special equipment?

No special equipment. A laptop works best, phone if that's all you have. Earphones are a must.

How quickly will I notice a difference?

You'll notice something in your first session. That's what the guarantee is based on. Real, consistent progress after that comes week by week, and it moves faster the more you practice between sessions.
