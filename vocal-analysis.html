The Patterns We Detect

Vocal Tension

The muscles around the larynx are working harder than they need to. Very common, and very fixable.

Chest Voice Ceiling

Singing entirely in chest voice. The upper register is there, it just hasn't been trained yet.

Airy Voice

Air escaping on every note. Limits projection, dynamics, and endurance.

Pitch Drift

Pitch moves around on sustained notes, a coordination issue, not an ear problem.

Solid Foundation

Strong base. The next level is about refinement, consistency, and upper-range expansion.

Questions
Is this actually free?
Yes. No credit card, no account, no trial. You send the clips, you get your Vocal Analysis. The only thing I ask is that you give it an honest shot, the better the recording, the better the analysis.
How long until I get my Vocal Analysis?
The test itself takes about 6 minutes. The analysis runs automatically after you submit (real audio processing, not a quiz). Your results show up on the page within about 20 seconds of submitting (your recordings are analyzed as you go), and the PDF lands in your inbox right after.
What if my recording quality isn't great?
Your laptop or phone's built-in mic in a reasonably quiet room is enough. No need for studio equipment. The analysis works on the pitch and phonation data, not production quality.
What happens after I get my Vocal Analysis?
Nothing, unless you want it to. The report stands on its own. If you want to go deeper, there's a link to book a first session, but there's no pressure and no follow-up emails.
I've had lessons before and nothing worked. Will this be different?
That's exactly who this is for. Most lessons work on technique without identifying the underlying pattern. The Vocal Analysis names the specific mechanism, which changes everything about how you train.
