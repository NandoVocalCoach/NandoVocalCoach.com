The Patterns We Detect

The Clencher

Neck tension grips on higher notes. The most common pattern in untrained singers.

The Floater

Real range — but navigating around the bridge instead of crossing it.

Chest-Locked

Singing entirely in chest voice. The top half of your voice is unused.

Breathy

Folds not fully closing. Limits projection, dynamics, and endurance.

Pitch Instability

Pitch moves around on sustained notes — a coordination issue, not an ear problem.

Developing

Solid foundation. The next level is about refinement and consistency.
